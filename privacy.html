Who we are

NairaExchangeRate.com is run by PluxelSystems Limited, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for this website. You can contact us at nairaexchanger@gmail.com.

What data we collect

You can use this site without creating an account or giving us any personal details.

  • Visitor statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and gives us only totals (for example, pages viewed, country and device type), not information about you as an individual.
  • Hosting. Our host, Netlify, processes standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page and keep the site secure.
  • Services your browser loads. To show live rates, fonts and icons, your browser connects to third-party services: a public exchange-rate data feed (served via jsDelivr and Cloudflare Pages), Google Fonts and Cloudflare's cdnjs. Like any website request, these services can see your IP address. We do not send them any other information about you.
  • If you email us. We keep your email and message only as long as needed to reply, and we never sell or share it.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not run advertising trackers. The lawful basis for the limited processing above is our legitimate interest in running and improving a free information website.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies, and our visitor statistics are cookie-free, so we do not show a cookie banner. If you follow a link to another website, such as a partner, WhatsApp, X or Facebook, that website may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

If we add advertising or any tool that uses cookies in future, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

About our exchange rates

  • Official CBN rate: published by the Central Bank of Nigeria, collected from a public data feed.
  • UK parallel (dealer) rate: the daily rate quoted by a UK money-transfer dealer, shown with permission and with the time it was last updated.
  • Other mid-market rates: daily reference rates from a public exchange-rate data feed.

Rates are for information only and are not financial advice or an offer to buy or sell currency. Banks, bureaux de change and dealers will quote their own prices. Always confirm the rate and the bank details with your provider before you send money. NairaExchangeRate.com never holds, sends or receives money for anyone.

News headlines link to the original publishers. Summaries are written by us; the full articles belong to their publishers.
