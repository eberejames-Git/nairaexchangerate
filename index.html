    Questions people ask

    What is the black market (parallel) rate?

    It's the rate street dealers and money transfer agents charge outside the official banking system. It's usually weaker than the official CBN rate, so you get more Naira for each pound or dollar.

    Why are there two dealer rates?

    Dealers buy pounds cheaper than they sell them. "You send £" is what you receive in Naira when you send pounds home. "You pay ₦" is what you pay in Naira to get pounds in the UK. The gap between them is the dealer's margin.

    How often are the rates updated?

    Mid-market rates refresh every morning. The UK dealer rate updates each afternoon once dealers publish their price, and always shows when it was last changed.

    Can I buy or sell currency through this site?

    No. This site publishes rates for information only and never handles money. Always confirm the rate and the bank account with your provider before you pay.
